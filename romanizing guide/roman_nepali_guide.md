# Roman Nepali: A Complete Romanization and Typing Guide

*A practical writing standard for converting spoken and written Nepali into the Latin alphabet.*

## 1. Understanding Roman Nepali

Roman Nepali is the practice of writing Nepali words using the English (Latin) alphabet instead of Devanagari.

It is commonly used in:

- WhatsApp and Messenger conversations.
- TikTok, Instagram and Facebook comments.
- Social media captions and posts.
- Online discussions and informal communication.
- Digital communication where typing in Devanagari is inconvenient.

Unlike formal transliteration systems, casual Roman Nepali prioritizes pronunciation, readability and typing convenience.

### Three important principles

1. **Pronunciation over spelling:** Write words according to how Nepali speakers pronounce them.
2. **Consistency over perfection:** Use the same spelling for the same word throughout a document.
3. **Readability over shortcuts:** Prefer spellings that other Nepali speakers can immediately understand.

### Three levels of Roman Nepali

| Level | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Readable Roman Nepali | General writing | `ke chha?` |
| Casual Roman Nepali | Everyday conversations | `k cha?` |
| Ultra-casual Roman Nepali | Fast texting | `k xa?` |

The default throughout this guide is readable Roman Nepali, with casual alternatives included where appropriate.

## 2. Basic Writing Rules

| Rule | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Alphabet | Use lowercase | `ma`, `timi`, `ghar` |
| Characters | Plain English letters (a–z) | `khabar` |
| Diacritics | Avoid special marks | `th`, not `ṭh` |
| Pronunciation | Follow spoken Nepali | `ramro` |
| Vowel length | Use doubled vowels when necessary | `pani` / `paani` |
| Word endings | Avoid unnecessary final vowels | `ghar`, `kam` |
| English words | Retain English spelling | `phone`, `video` |
| Names | Use established spellings | `Kathmandu` |
| Consistency | Choose one spelling | `chha`, not alternating with `xa` |

**Important:** Roman Nepali is not English written phonetically. Nepali grammar and vocabulary remain Nepali even when written using English characters.

## 3. Vowel Romanization

Nepali has 11 traditional vowel letters. In casual Roman Nepali, some distinctions are simplified.

| Devanagari | Roman | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| अ | `a` | `ghar` | House |
| आ | `a` / `aa` | `kaam` | Work |
| इ | `i` | `timi` | You |
| ई | `i` | `didi` | Older sister |
| उ | `u` | `suru` | Beginning |
| ऊ | `u` | `phool` | Flower |
| ऋ | `ri` | `ritu` | Season |
| ए | `e` | `keta` | Boy |
| ऐ | `ai` | `bhai` | Brother |
| ओ | `o` | `bol` | Speak |
| औ | `au` | `nau` | Nine |

### Vowel length

Long and short vowels are often written identically in casual Roman Nepali.

However, doubling vowels can help distinguish words with different meanings.

| Roman | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `pani` | पनि | Also |
| `paani` | पानी | Water |
| `kam` | कम | Less |
| `kaam` | काम | Work |

**Recommended convention:**

- Use single vowels by default.
- Use `aa` when vowel length helps distinguish words.
- Do not randomly alternate between `a` and `aa`.

### Diphthongs

Some vowel combinations should remain recognizable.

| Sound | Roman | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ऐ | `ai` | `bhai` |
| औ | `au` | `nau` |

## 4. Consonant Romanization

This is where consistency becomes particularly important.

Nepali has several consonants that sound similar to English speakers but represent different sounds in Nepali.

### 4.1 Basic consonants

| Devanagari | Roman | Example |
|---|---|---|
| क | `k` | `kam` |
| ग | `g` | `ghar` |
| ङ | `ng` | `anga` |
| च | `ch` | `chiso` |
| ज | `j` | `janu` |
| ञ | `ny` | `gyan` (conventional spelling) |
| ट | `t` | `tika` |
| ड | `d` | `danda` |
| ण | `n` | `ganesh` |
| त | `t` | `timi` |
| द | `d` | `din` |
| न | `n` | `nam` |
| प | `p` | `pani` |
| ब | `b` | `bato` |
| म | `m` | `manche` |
| य | `y` | `yo` |
| र | `r` | `ramro` |
| ल | `l` | `lamo` |
| व | `w` / `b` | `wa` / `bato` |
| श | `sh` | `shanti` |
| ष | `sh` | `bishesh` |
| स | `s` | `sathi` |
| ह | `h` | `hari` |

Some conventional spellings, particularly names and Sanskrit-derived words, may not follow a purely phonetic representation.

### 4.2 Aspirated consonants

Aspiration means producing a consonant with an additional burst of air.

In Roman Nepali, this is generally represented by adding `h`.

| Devanagari | Roman | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ख | `kh` | `khana` |
| घ | `gh` | `ghar` |
| छ | `chh` | `chhori` |
| झ | `jh` | `jhyal` |
| ठ | `th` | `thulo` |
| ढ | `dh` | `dhunga` |
| थ | `th` | `thaha` |
| ध | `dh` | `dhanyabad` |
| फ | `ph` / `f` | `phool` |
| भ | `bh` | `bhai` |

**Important distinction:** Do not automatically remove `h` from aspirated consonants.

For example:

- `k` and `kh` are different sounds.
- `t` and `th` are different sounds.
- `d` and `dh` are different sounds.
- `p` and `ph` are different sounds.

Casual typing sometimes ignores these differences, but preserving them makes Roman Nepali easier to understand.

### 4.3 Special consonant combinations

| Devanagari | Roman | Example |
|---|---|---|
| क्ष | `ksh` / `chh` | `kshama` |
| त्र | `tr` | `mitra` |
| ज्ञ | `gya` / `gy` | `gyan` |
| श्र | `shr` | `shram` |
| द्य | `dy` | `vidya` |
| द्व | `dw` | `dwanda` |

For established names, preserve their conventional spelling rather than forcing a phonetic conversion.

## 5. The Special Case of छ

The letter छ is one of the most inconsistently written characters in everyday Roman Nepali.

Three common spellings are:

| Spelling | Example | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| `chha` | `ke chha?` | More explicit |
| `cha` | `ke cha?` | Common casual spelling |
| `xa` | `k xa?` | Very informal texting |

For this guide, the recommended default is **`chha`**.

- `ch` represents च.
- `chh` represents छ.

This keeps a clearer distinction between the two Nepali sounds.

The same convention should apply to related verb forms.

| Meaning | Readable | Casual |
|---|---|---|
| Is | `chha` | `cha` |
| Is not | `chhaina` | `chaina` |
| Does | `garchha` | `garcha` |
| Does not | `gardaina` | `gardaina` |
| They do | `garchhan` | `garchan` |

Use one style consistently within a piece of writing.

## 8. Postpositions and Word Joining

Nepali uses postpositions rather than English-style prepositions.

Some grammatical elements are joined to pronouns, while others remain separate.

### Joined forms

| Roman | Devanagari |
|---|---|
| `malai` | मलाई |
| `timilai` | तिमीलाई |
| `tapailai` | तपाईंलाई |
| `maile` | मैले |
| `timile` | तिमीले |
| `usko` | उसको |
| `hamro` | हाम्रो |
| `uniharuko` | उनीहरूको |

### Separate forms

| Roman | Example |
|---|---|
| `ma` | `ghar ma` |
| `bata` | `school bata` |
| `sanga` | `sathi sanga` |
| `dekhi` | `aja dekhi` |
| `samma` | `Kathmandu samma` |
| `ko lagi` | `timro lagi` |
| `ko barema` | `yo kura ko barema` |

**Rule:** Join grammatical endings to pronouns when they form a conventional combined word. Keep postpositions separate from ordinary nouns.

## 9. Verb Romanization

Verbs are one of the most important parts of Roman Nepali because their endings change according to person, politeness and tense.

### 9.1 Present tense

Using गर्नु (to do):

| Person | Devanagari | Roman |
|---|---|---|
| I | म गर्छु | `ma garchhu` |
| You (informal) | तिमी गर्छौ | `timi garchhau` |
| You (polite) | तपाईं गर्नुहुन्छ | `tapai garnuhunchha` |
| He / she | ऊ गर्छ | `u garchha` |
| We | हामी गर्छौँ | `hami garchhau` |
| They | उनीहरू गर्छन् | `uniharu garchhan` |

Casual alternatives include `garchu`, `garchau`, `garcha` and `garchan`.

### 9.2 Past tense

| Person | Devanagari | Roman |
|---|---|---|
| I | मैले गरेँ | `maile gare` |
| You | तिमीले गर्‍यौ | `timile garyau` |
| He / she | उसले गर्‍यो | `usle garyo` |
| She (marked feminine form) | उसले गरिन् | `usle garin` |
| They | उनीहरूले गरे | `uniharule gare` |

Note that Nepali past-tense forms depend on the verb, grammatical person, gender in some constructions and sentence structure. The table shows common examples, not a universal conjugation pattern.

## 10. English and Nepali Code-Switching

Mixing English and Nepali is completely natural in digital communication.

The main rule is to retain English spelling while applying Nepali grammar naturally.

| Correct | Avoid |
|---|---|
| `video herna` | `vidiou herna` |
| `message gare` | `mesij gare` |
| `phone ma` | `fon ma` |
| `reel banayo` | `ril banayo` |
| `file pathaideu` | `fail pathaideu` |

### English words with Nepali endings

- `phone haru`
- `video ko lagi`
- `reel ma`
- `message garna`
- `post bata`
- `computer chalauna`

Do not unnecessarily modify English words to make them look Nepali.

## 12. Punctuation and Sentence Structure

Roman Nepali generally follows the same punctuation conventions as English.

| Purpose | Example |
|---|---|
| Full stop | `ma ghar janchu.` |
| Question | `timi kaha chhau?` |
| Exclamation | `kati ramro!` |
| Comma | `ho, ma aauchhu.` |
| Quotation | `usle bhanyo, "ma aauchhu."` |

### Sentence structure

Keep Nepali sentence order rather than translating English sentence structure directly.

Examples:

- `ma aja Kathmandu janchu.`
- `timi le khana khayau?`
- `malai yo kura thaha chaina.`
- `bholi bhetaula hai.`

## 16. Casual Roman Nepali vs. Formal Romanization

The conventions above are designed for practical everyday communication. They are not a reversible or academically standardized transliteration system.

For linguistic research, dictionaries, official documents and language-processing applications, more precise systems are preferable.

Two established systems worth distinguishing are:

- **ISO 15919:** A standardized transliteration system for Indic scripts, including Devanagari. It preserves distinctions that casual Roman Nepali normally removes.
- **IAST:** A scholarly transliteration convention commonly used for Sanskrit and related textual material.

For example, casual Roman Nepali may write `t` for both त and ट, whereas a scholarly system distinguishes them.

This distinction matters if the goal is to convert Roman Nepali back into Devanagari accurately.

---

## 17. Final Rules to Remember

1. Write how Nepali is pronounced.
2. Use plain English letters.
3. Preserve meaningful consonant distinctions where possible.
4. Use `ch` for च and `chh` for छ.
5. Use `aa` only when it improves clarity.
6. Preserve English words in their original spelling.
7. Keep grammatical endings and postpositions consistent.
8. Do not unnecessarily add vowels at the ends of words.
9. Preserve established spellings of names and places.
10. Never randomly switch between different spellings of the same word.

**The ultimate objective:** Anyone who understands spoken Nepali should be able to read Roman Nepali naturally without having to mentally translate every word.

## 18. Building a More Complete Roman Nepali Standard

For a genuinely reliable Roman Nepali standard, keep two clearly separate conventions:

### Casual Roman Nepali

Optimized for:

- WhatsApp and Messenger.
- Social media comments.
- Everyday conversations.
- Fast informal typing.

It allows shortcuts, simplified spellings and common texting variants.

### Standardized Roman Nepali

Optimized for:

- Consistent spelling.
- Dictionaries and educational material.
- Language datasets.
- Transliteration tools.
- Conversion back to Devanagari.

It uses a defined character-to-sound mapping, consistent vowel conventions and documented exceptions.

The guide above primarily establishes a readable, standardized house style while acknowledging everyday alternatives. A complete technical standard would additionally need a full character mapping, rules for conjunct consonants, schwa deletion, loanwords, dialect variation and ambiguity resolution.
